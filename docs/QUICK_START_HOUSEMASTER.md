# Housemaster — one-page quick start

**Goal:** run your house's roll call and exeat requests without bypassing the
workflow.

## Roll call

1. Open **Roll call** and select your house and the correct session.
2. Mark every student. Use **On Exeat** for an approved pass, **Infirmary** for
   sick bay, and **Activity** for an official school activity.
3. Check the roster count and complete the session. Completed entries lock.
4. For a mistake after completion, open **Attendance corrections**, record the
   new status, reason, and evidence. Present ↔ Absent needs a second approver.
5. You cannot approve your own correction.

## Exeats

1. Open **Exeats → New request**. Complete the destination, dates, reason, and
   guardian contact.
2. Submit the request. A Housemaster can initiate but does not authorize a
   general emergency movement.
3. For an approved pass, use **Reschedule** if times change. Record the reason.
   Rescheduling stops working after gate departure.
4. Do not approve a request outside your house or a close relative's request.

## Late returns

1. You receive an in-app alert as soon as the expected return is missed. Contact
   the guardian and record the contact attempt.
2. Open **Operations → Late returns** and review the incident.
3. At two hours late the Senior Housemaster is notified and a written incident
   is created. At six hours the Headmaster is notified.
4. Keep the incident open until the facts and follow-up are recorded.

## Disputes

Open **Disputes → New dispute**, select the exact record, and describe the
issue. A different senior reviewer decides it. Do not alter the record first.

## Safety rules

- No closed-session edits through roll-call screens or Django admin.
- No deletion of duplicate sessions or attendance evidence.
- No overcapacity placement without a written, time-bound senior exception.
- Keep medical details out of general notes and SMS.
