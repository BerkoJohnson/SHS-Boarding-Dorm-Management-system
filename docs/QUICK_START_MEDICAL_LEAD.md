# Medical / Infirmary Lead — one-page quick start

**Goal:** authorize only medical movements within your scope.

1. Sign in with your named medical account.
2. Open **Exeats → Pending approvals**. The queue is limited to **Medical**
   passes; ordinary town, weekend, vacation, and general emergency passes are
   outside your authority.
3. Check the student, destination, clinical urgency (without putting clinical
   detail in the reason field), times, and guardian contact.
4. Approve or reject with a clear reason. Never approve an ordinary or general
   emergency leave pass; escalate those to the Headmaster/Senior Housemaster.
5. For a medical override, record the immediate reason. The Headmaster/ADMIN or
   Senior Housemaster is notified; the 24-hour review must be completed by
   someone other than the authorizer.
6. Use **Reschedule** only for a pass in your medical scope before gate
   departure.
7. Keep medical details out of SMS, general notes, dispute summaries, and
   exported reports unless the school has approved that specific use.

**Escalate** suspected abuse, a request outside medical scope, or a conflict of
interest to the Headmaster. Do not use another staff member's account.
