# Senior Housemaster — one-page quick start

**Goal:** authorize, review, and correct only within the approved policy.

## Daily review queue

1. Open **Exeat Reviews** and check emergency overrides awaiting the 24-hour
   review. You cannot review an override you authorized.
2. Open **Late Returns** and review incidents escalated to you. Confirm guardian
   and Housemaster contact, then record the outcome.
3. Open **Attendance Corrections** and decide pending requests. Check reason and
   evidence; Present ↔ Absent and conduct-linked changes need this second
   approval. You cannot approve your own request.
4. Open **Disputes** and decide cases you did not raise. Record a clear outcome
   and decision note.
5. Open **Gate Duty Roster** to assign or cancel temporary gate cover. Cancel
   expired cover promptly.

## Capacity exceptions

1. Open **Capacity Exceptions**.
2. Approve only a written, time-bound exception of **seven days or less**.
3. Record the reason and the extra places. Review weekly.
4. Do not create a permanent exception or place a student over capacity without
   one.

## Emergency movements

- A Housemaster initiates; the Headmaster/ADMIN or Senior Housemaster
  authorizes.
- Record the reason immediately.
- Ensure a 24-hour review is completed by someone other than the authorizer.
- A medical lead authorizes medical movements within their scope.

## Rules

- Never edit a completed roll call or gate timestamp directly.
- Never delete an incident, correction, or dispute to make a queue disappear.
- Escalate to the Headmaster when policy requires it; do not invent a new
  approval path.
