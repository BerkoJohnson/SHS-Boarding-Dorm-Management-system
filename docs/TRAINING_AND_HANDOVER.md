## Joint training session plan

Run separate 45-minute sessions for gate staff, Housemasters/tutors, and senior
leaders/administrators. Each session should use a staging tenant and cover:

1. Sign-in with a named account and the correct house/term.
2. One complete gate departure and return, including a late return.
3. One roll call, including an absence and a post-closure correction request.
4. One exeat request, approval, rejection, and reschedule.
5. One medical-scope approval and one general emergency override with review.
6. One dispute opened, reviewed, and decided by a different person.
7. One CSV, XLSX, and PDF export; explain the export audit log.
8. What to do when the screen is slow, the network fails, or a record looks
   wrong.

Record attendance, questions, and follow-up actions below. Keep this record
with the school's training evidence.

## Competency check

A participant is ready for the pilot when they can, without prompting:

- complete a gate departure and return and explain why they must not repeat a
  movement;
- complete a roll call and explain how to request a correction after closure;
- approve or reject an exeat only within their jurisdiction;
- explain the 2-hour/6-hour late-return escalation;
- open a dispute and explain why the raiser cannot decide it;
- find the export audit log and explain the D1 retention rule;
- state the emergency numbers and the person to call when the system is
  unavailable.
